MedMart Color System

What each color is for

Every color in the MedMart palette has a specific role. This keeps the interface consistent and professional.

MedMart typography rule: Manrope → headings, hero text, section titles, product names Inter → paragraphs, buttons, navigation, forms, labels

Primary Blue

#2775E4

BRAND

The main MedMart brand color. Use it for primary buttons, links, active states, icons and important actions.

Best for: Buttons · Links · Logo · Active states

Healthcare Teal

#08AEBC

ACCENT

Adds the healthcare personality to MedMart. Use it for secondary actions, medical icons, highlights and accents.

Best for: Healthcare · Icons · Badges · Highlights

Dark Teal

#058A98

INTERACTION

The darker version of the healthcare teal. Primarily used for hover states and stronger teal accents.

Best for: Hover · Focus · Dark accents

Hero Background

#E9F3FE

BACKGROUND

The primary soft background for large sections, especially the landing-page hero.

Best for: Hero · Large sections · Page backgrounds

Decorative Blue

#B1D0FB

DECORATION

A stronger soft blue used to create depth and decorative shapes behind images or content.

Best for: Shapes · Blobs · Image backgrounds

Soft Surface

#DBEBFB

SURFACE

Used to separate content without relying heavily on borders or dark colors.

Best for: Cards · Inputs · Categories · Sections

Primary Text

#171E26

TEXT

The main text color for headings, navigation, product names and important information.

Best for: Headings · Navigation · Important text
